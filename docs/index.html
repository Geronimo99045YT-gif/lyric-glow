Features

Everything a desk setup display needs.

Open it, play a song, and it just works. No fiddling with timings or skins.

🎤

Karaoke lyrics

Synced lyrics that fill in word by word, with the previous and next lines faded around the current one.

💿

Full-height vinyl

A record with the album cover as its label spins while the song plays, with a tonearm that creeps in as it progresses.

⚡

Beat-reactive glow

Edge lighting in the cover's dominant colour that flashes on every beat. One checkbox turns the flashing off.

🌊

Sound wave

Flowing layered waves driven by the real audio spectrum, drawn behind the lyrics.

🎛️

Player controls

Play, pause and skip from the screen. Works with any player on your PC, or Spotify directly if you're on Premium.

🎨

Themes

Classic, Neon, Vinyl shop or Minimal. Each changes the fonts, colours, glow and layout together. Plus eight lyric entrance styles, including a 3D wheel that really spins.

📺

Streaming overlay

A transparent lyrics-only window for Discord or games, or a link for an OBS browser source. Drag it anywhere.

🔎

Pick the right lyrics

Wrong or missing lyrics? Search the database yourself and choose the right version. It remembers your pick for that song.

🔌

Auto-detects what's playing

The Windows app reads Spotify, YouTube, Apple Music and more. No account, no setup. Or connect Spotify for the full thing.

How it works

Up and running in a minute.

Get it

Download the Windows app and double-click it. Or open the web version right here in your browser.

Pick your player

In the app, hit "Skip" to read your PC's player. Or connect your own free Spotify app for exact progress and BPM.

Press play

Lyrics, cover art and the beat flash start on their own. Hit F for fullscreen and put it on a second monitor.

Which one?

Windows app or web version.

The app is the easy full-featured one. The web version works anywhere, but a website can't see your other apps.
